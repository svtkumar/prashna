Syllabus

What this exam covers.

  • Electrostatics — charges, fields, Gauss's law, potential, capacitance
  • Current Electricity — Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's rules, Wheatstone bridge
  • Moving Charges and Magnetism — Biot–Savart, Ampère, force between conductors
  • Magnetism and Matter — bar magnet, magnetic moment, materials
  • Electromagnetic Induction — Faraday, Lenz, self and mutual inductance
  • Alternating Current — LCR, impedance, resonance, transformers
  • Electromagnetic Waves — characteristics and spectrum
  • Ray Optics — mirrors, lenses, prisms, total internal reflection, instruments
  • Wave Optics — interference, Young's double slit, diffraction
  • Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter — photoelectric effect, de Broglie
  • Atoms and Nuclei — Bohr model, spectral series, binding energy
  • Semiconductor Electronics — energy bands, p and n type, p-n junction, diode as rectifier
