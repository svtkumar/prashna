Syllabus

What this exam covers.

  • Solutions — concentration, Raoult's law, colligative properties, van't Hoff factor
  • Electrochemistry — galvanic cells, Nernst equation, conductance, Kohlrausch's law
  • Chemical Kinetics — rate laws, order, integrated equations, Arrhenius
  • d- and f-Block Elements — trends, oxidation states, KMnO₄ and K₂Cr₂O₇, lanthanoids
  • Coordination Compounds — nomenclature, isomerism, valence bond and crystal field theory
  • Haloalkanes and Haloarenes — SN1 and SN2, stereochemistry
  • Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers — preparation, reactions, acidity
  • Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids — nucleophilic addition, aldol, Cannizzaro
  • Amines — basicity, diazonium salts
  • Biomolecules — carbohydrates, proteins, nucleic acids, vitamins
